Recipe of the Week

Nigerian Jollof Rice

Jollof Rice is one of the most popular dishes in Nigeria. It is prepared with rice, tomatoes, peppers, onions, vegetable oil, seasoning and spices. It is commonly served with chicken, beef, fish, or salad.

Ingredients

  • 4 cups of Rice
  • Fresh Tomatoes
  • Tomato Paste
  • Red Bell Pepper
  • Scotch Bonnet Pepper
  • Onions
  • Vegetable Oil
  • Curry Powder
  • Thyme
  • Seasoning Cubes
  • Salt
  • Chicken Stock

Kitchen Equipment Needed

  1. Cooking Pot
  2. Gas Cooker or Stove
  3. Kitchen Knife
  4. Wooden Spoon
  5. Blender
  6. Measuring Cup
  7. Serving Spoon

Preparation Steps

Step Description
1 Wash and blend tomatoes, pepper, and onions.
2 Heat vegetable oil and fry the tomato mixture.
3 Add tomato paste, seasoning, curry, thyme, and salt.
4 Add chicken stock and allow to boil.
5 Add washed rice and cook until soft.
6 Serve with chicken and salad.

Nutrition Information

Nutrient Amount (Approx.)
Calories 450 kcal
Protein 10 g
Carbohydrates 60 g
Fat 15 g

Cooking Tips

  • Wash the rice before cooking.
  • Use fresh ingredients for better taste.
  • Do not overcook the rice.
  • Allow the rice to steam after cooking.
  • Serve while hot.
